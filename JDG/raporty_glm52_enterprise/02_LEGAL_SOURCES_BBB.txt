SLO_SLA
-------
[POTWIERDZONE_KODEM] ETAP 02 applies the following safety objectives:
- source hash mismatch: immediate FAIL;
- invalid or overlapping effective interval: FAIL;
- missing provenance path: FAIL;
- orphaned Legal Twin source: warning in structural mode and BLOCK in publication mode;
- official snapshot required for publication: yes;
- two distinct reviewers required: yes;
- repository text as sole evidence: forbidden;
- unverified source publication: forbidden.
[POTWIERDZONE_KODEM] Existing enterprise SLO/SLA remain in the master contract;
this stage adds source-integrity gates and does not claim external-feed latency.

PASS_FAIL_GATES
---------------
[POTWIERDZONE_KODEM] `legal_source_registry.py validate` blocks malformed records,
source-hash tampering, invalid dates, missing provenance and broken node links.
[POTWIERDZONE_KODEM] `--publication-gate` additionally blocks every candidate
without an official snapshot hash, `PUBLISHED` state and two distinct approvals.
[POTWIERDZONE_KODEM] The SQL publication view in migration 005 independently
requires an official snapshot and at least two distinct reviewers for PASS.
[POTWIERDZONE_KODEM] Legal diffs are never self-publishing: `diff_registries()`
marks every changed source with `requires_4_eyes_review=true` and returns
`BLOCKED_UNTIL_REVIEW`.

LEGAL_CARTOGRAPHY_AND_GAPS
---------------------------
[POTWIERDZONE_KODEM] The pipeline preserves the distinction between an act, a
publication citation, an article node, a rule link and an external official text.
[POTWIERDZONE_KODEM] Missing article-level official snapshots, amendment chains,
exceptions, forms, interpretations and accounting-document contents are not
fabricated. They remain review inputs for external ingest and later stages.
[LUKA] `legal_reference_canon.json` is a repository catalogue, not an official
ISAP/RCL text archive. Its Dz.U. citations and current validity still require
external verification.
[LUKA] Existing `legal_graph.json` contains inherited historical claims that are
now labelled unverified in the registry; this stage does not silently correct the
substantive law.
[LUKA] The existing crawler stores a preview-oriented cache and is not promoted
as proof of a signed official snapshot. A future ingest adapter must persist the
full source bytes, retrieval metadata and official hash before publication.
[LUKA] Interpretation databases (KIS/MF), form schemas and all amendment-level
exceptions require dedicated source adapters and independent review.
